Our channel publishing app

Our private production tool prepares the channel’s own AI-generated kids videos for its own accounts. After the channel operator approves a video and caption, the planned TikTok integration uploads it as an inbox draft. The operator opens TikTok’s mobile app, reviews the caption and AI-generated label, and chooses whether to publish. TikTok integration is currently disabled pending developer approval and account authorization.

The app is operated by the channel team. It does not accept outside users, collect children’s information, or access other creators’ accounts. This website provides information and policies; it does not offer public account connections.
