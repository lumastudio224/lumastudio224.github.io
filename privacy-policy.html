Privacy policy

This policy covers the Luma and friends channel publishing app and this informational website. Contact: lumastudio224@gmail.com.

Information we use

With the channel operator’s authorization, the app stores the channel’s TikTok account identifier, basic profile information if needed to confirm the connected account, OAuth credentials, upload status, and its own video files, captions, and approval records. We do not onboard third-party users, request followers, messages, comments, or analytics, or collect personal information from children.

Purpose and sharing

Data is used to authenticate the operator, upload the channel’s own AI-generated videos as TikTok inbox drafts, and report upload status. Videos and necessary authorization data are sent to TikTok through its official APIs. Operator approval and status messages use Telegram; caption preparation uses the existing Gemini service. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Storage, control, and deletion

Credentials are stored in restricted authorized credential storage and excluded from public repositories and code backups. Production files and approval history are retained by the channel operator for channel operations. Tokens are retained while the connection is needed and removed on disconnection or an operator deletion request. The operator may revoke authorization in TikTok and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion. TikTok independently controls copies uploaded to its service under its own policies.

Website and OAuth callback

This static site contains no analytics, advertising trackers, third-party scripts, or forms. GitHub Pages, when deployed, may process basic request metadata under GitHub’s policies. The callback page only displays the authorization code and state locally in your browser; it does not forward them to any service. The hosting provider receives the initial page request, which may contain OAuth query parameters; the page removes those parameters from browser history after reading them. Only the operator should use this callback.
